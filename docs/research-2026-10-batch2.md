# Research trail, October 2026, second batch (drafts)

Where every room name and fact on the new pages came from (checked 18 October 2026). Booking-site pages were read as text; the names below are copied as shown there.

## Colosseum (Rome)
- Palazzo Manfredi, Via Labicana 125: Hotels.com lists 13 categories https://hotels.com/ho227871/palazzo-manfredi-small-luxury-hotels-of-the-world-rome-italy . Colosseum in the name: Master Colosseum View, Colosseo Suite, Grand View Colosseo Suite, Ludus Colosseum View. Every category with a view is labelled "City view" there. Other categories: Executive Double Room, Grand View Junior Suite, Grand View Gallery Suite, Grand View Penthouse Suite, Prestige Double Room, Lateran Gallery Suite, Family Suite, two apartments in the annex building 400 m away. Photo captions: Junior Suite ("window overlooking the Colosseum at night"), Family Suite ("window with a view of the Colosseum"). The Hotel Guru: Executive Double Rooms overlook Colle Oppio and the Domus Aurea https://thehotelguru.com/best-hotels-in/italy/rome/views . Untold Italy recommends the Grand View Penthouse Suite, "most rooms look out onto the monument" https://untolditaly.com/hotels-near-the-colosseum-rome . Michelin Guide: Via Labicana 125, 16 rooms (fourteen rooms and two suites), no room names https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hotels-stays/rome/hotel-palazzo-manfredi-6280 . Five Star Alliance names a "Colosseum Suite" (first floor, view from four windows) and a "Ludus Magnus Suite" (ground floor) https://www.fivestaralliance.com/node/25169 : names differ from Hotels.com, not used on the page.
- Mercure Roma Centro Colosseo, Via Labicana 144, 4 stars on Hotels.com: https://hotels.com/ho126867/mercure-roma-centro-colosseo-rome-italy . Five categories: Classic Single Room, Superior Double Room, 1 Double Bed, Superior Twin Room, Privilege, Double Room, and "Superior Double Room (with view on the Colosseum)". Hotel site https://all.accor.com/hotel/2909/index.en.shtml : five categories, "Superior double room with Colosseum view", view type "Historic side view"; the extras text mentions 19 Superior rooms with Colosseum views (unclear whether all are in that category, so the number is not on the page). Casper's Journey: not all rooms have the view https://caspersjourney.com/5-best-hotels-near-colosseum-in-rome-with-stunning-views/ .
- Colosseum Corner, Via del Monte Oppio 12: hotel site https://www.colosseumcorner.com/ lists Deluxe Double Room, Deluxe Double Room With Colosseum View, Deluxe Triple Room With Colosseum View (Colosseum and Domus Aurea).
- N° 9 Colosseo (also HT N°9 Colosseo), Piazza del Colosseo 9: Hotels.com https://www.hotels.com/ho388235/n-9-colosseo-rome-italy/ and Travelocity https://www.travelocity.com/Rome-Hotels-N-9-Colosseo.h4551510.Hotel-Information list "Double Room (with Coliseum View)" and "Family Suite". Travelocity says 4-star affittacamere, Hotels.com shows no star rating, so no stars on the page. She Wanders Abroad (rooms overlooking the monument and the Imperial Fora; Double and Suite) https://shewandersabroad.com/recommends/ht-n9-colosseo/ .
- Colosseo Panoramic Rooms, Via Nicola Salvi 68 (level reported): Expedia lists "Double Room" (city view) and "Classic Double Room, Balcony, Courtyard View"; text: select rooms have panoramic windows over the Colosseum and Roman Forum https://www.expedia.com/Rome-Hotels-Colosseo-Panoramic-Rooms.h9368658.Hotel-Information . Oyster: three of four rooms see the Colosseum from the side; "Standard Room with Colosseum View and Big Bathroom (Room 2)" and "Standard Room with Internal View (Room 3)" are names from its text, not sold categories; rooms assigned first come, first served https://www.oyster.com/rome/hotels/colosseo-panoramic-rooms/ .
- Hotel Colosseum, Via Sforza 10 (used in the "≠" pair only): Travelocity lists Double or Twin Room, Triple Room, Single Room, Double Room Single Use, Family Room, no Colosseum view in any name https://www.travelocity.com/Rome-Hotels-Hotel-Colosseum.h68623.Hotel-Information ; Jones Around the World says the view is from the panoramic terrace https://www.jonesaroundtheworld.com/rome-hotels-with-view-of-colosseum/ .
- Hotel Capo d'Africa (alternatives block): Time Out, Colosseum view from the roof-level breakfast area and terrace, no room named https://www.timeout.com/rome/hotels/capo-dafrica . Hotels.com lists Superior Double Room, Deluxe Room (With Double bed), Executive Room, Triple Room, Family Room, no view named.
- Not used: Palm Suite (Via del Colosseo 20; categories Palm Court View, Family Court View, Palm Suite Deluxe, Palm Forum View, Family Forum View, Grand Palm Suite on slh.com; a guest sees the edge of the Colosseum from a Forum View room; Michelin and SLH disagree on the room count), Room Mate Collection Mia (no view category; a guest review says the room had the view), apartments (47 Luxury Suites, Royal House, Giallo Colosseo, EVE Penthouse and similar: rentals, not hotel room categories), The Fifteen Keys Hotel (guide: rooms face the courtyard).
